NETWORK/01 — PAN-ODISHA ECOSYSTEM

THE OSCC COMMUNITY

Odisha Students Code Community isn't an elite club or a commercial training institute. It is an open, distributed movement built by students, for students, across engineering, diploma, and degree colleges in Odisha.

CORE/02 — GUIDING PRINCIPLES

WHAT WE STAND FOR

Six fundamental values that guide every repository, discussion, and initiative in OSCC.

01 // BUILD

REAL CODE

Learn by making real things. Theory is good; shipping working software to public GitHub repositories is transformative.

02 // CONTRIBUTE

OPEN COMMONS

Give back to open-source software. Build tools that make the next student's journey easier.

03 // COLLABORATE

CROSS-CAMPUS

Do not stay isolated in your own college hostel. Collaborate with motivated peers from Bhubaneswar to Sambalpur, Rourkela to Berhampur.

04 // LEARN

NO GATEKEEPING

Ask questions without fear. Nobody is expected to know everything. We respect curiosity above seniority.

05 // SHARE

OPEN OPPORTUNITIES

Share hackathons, internships, resources, and technical learnings freely. When one student succeeds, the whole community rises.

06 // GROW

PUBLIC PROOF

Replace resumes with public proof of work. Your merged PRs and commit history speak for themselves.

CAMPUS/03 — DECENTRALIZED STRUCTURE

COLLEGE CHAPTERS & STUDENT LEADS

Empowering campus leaders to bring the open-source culture to their local student bodies.

WHAT DOES A CAMPUS LEAD DO?
  • // Introduce fellow students to Git, GitHub, and OSCC open-source projects.
  • // Form campus hackathon teams and collaborative study groups.
  • // Host local Git contribution workshops and issue-solving jams.
  • // Represent their institution within the OSCC inter-college network.
START AN OSCC CHAPTER AT YOUR CAMPUS

WANT TO LEAD YOUR COLLEGE?

Any active student in Odisha passionate about open-source and peer learning can propose establishing an OSCC campus node. We provide mentoring, project templates, and community recognition.

TRUST/04 — ETHICS & SAFETY CHARTER

100% FREE, SAFE, AND TRANSPARENT

Student communities must protect their members. Here is our non-negotiable trust charter.

NO PAID BARRIERS

ALWAYS FREE TO JOIN

OSCC has no membership charges, no entry fees, and no paid premium tiers. Anyone asking for money under the OSCC brand is unauthorized.

NO SCAMS OR EXPLOITATION

VERIFIED OPPORTUNITIES

We do not sell student contact lists or promote dubious multi-level referral schemes. Opportunities shared are vetted for legitimacy.

ZERO HARASSMENT TOLERANCE

INCLUSIVE & RESPECTFUL

Discrimination, harassment, trolling, and intimidation are strictly prohibited across all our spaces under our Code of Conduct.

PUBLIC GOVERNANCE

OPEN SOURCE STEWARDSHIP

All code, documentation, and roadmap plans are open-source on GitHub, ensuring total public accountability.
